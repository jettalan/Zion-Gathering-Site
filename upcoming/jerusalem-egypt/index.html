For eleven days we walk the ground where scripture happened. This gathering moves through the Old and New Testament — the holy sites of Jerusalem and the Galilee where the Savior taught, suffered, and rose, and the land of Egypt where Israel was tried, preserved, and delivered. To stand in these places and open the scriptures is to read them with new eyes.

This trip was built around a sign in the heavens. On August 2, 2027, one of the longest total solar eclipses of the century will draw its shadow directly across Egypt — more than six minutes of midday darkness over that ancient land. We have timed these eleven days to put us beneath it. The scriptures speak again and again of the sun darkened and of wonders in the heavens above: “The sun shall be turned into darkness… before the great and terrible day of the Lord come” (Joel 2:31). We are going to witness one together, in the very part of the world where so much of the sacred record was written.

What to Expect

  • The holy sites of the Old and New Testament across Jerusalem, the Galilee, and Judea
  • The land of Egypt — the Nile, the pyramids, and the country of Israel’s bondage and exodus
  • A place beneath the great solar eclipse of August 2, 2027, in Egypt — over six minutes of totality
  • Scripture study on the ground, tied to each site we visit
  • Daily worship, testimony, and fellowship among a remnant community
  • Eleven days, two countries, one continuous walk through the scriptures
  • Lodging throughout included — airfare arranged by each traveler

Passports are required, and airfare is not included. Travelers book their own international flights. We will share recommended arrival cities, visa guidance, and the full day-by-day itinerary with everyone who reserves.
