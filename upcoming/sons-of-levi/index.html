“He is like a refiner’s fire, and like fullers’ soap: and he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver: and he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness.” — Malachi 3:2–3

This August we are calling on men — old and young — to gather for a weekend focused on purification, sanctification, priesthood power, and becoming true servants of the Lord. This is not just another retreat. This gathering is centered on the refining of the sons of Levi.

We will dive deep into the baptism of the Holy Ghost, exercising power in the priesthood, healing blessings and proper priesthood administration, sanctification as priesthood holders, the return of priesthood power, and the priesthood spoken of in Doctrine and Covenants 124 and how it has been restored.

This gathering is for all men. We will have men in attendance born in 1938 and 1944, alongside young men born in the early 2000s. Fathers, sons, grandfathers, brothers, friends — no age restrictions. If you desire to draw closer to the Lord, learn how to access priesthood power, become sanctified as a servant, understand healing and blessings, or prepare for what is coming, this gathering is for you.

What to Expect

  • Powerful scripture deep dives
  • Speakers you have seen on Zion Media
  • Priesthood instruction — how to access it, how to administer it
  • Outdoor activities and hiking
  • Basketball, pickleball, and fellowship
  • Worship and a sacrament-style meeting on Sunday
  • Brotherhood and connection

Check-in: Thursday evening between 5 and 6 PM. Full venue, schedule, and location details will be released soon.
