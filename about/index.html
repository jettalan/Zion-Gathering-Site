Our Story

About The Zion Gathering

Some things cannot be streamed.

How It Began

Nobody planned it. A group of people who had been studying together online reached a point where the screen was no longer enough. They wanted to sit across from each other, share a table, and have the conversations that do not fit in a comment thread.

So they found a place, made the calls, and showed up.

What happened over those few days was more than any of them expected. People had experiences they had quietly been waiting years for. Conversations unfolded that changed the direction of people's lives. When it ended, the only real question was how soon they could do it again.

That is where this started.

Why We Gather

Zion is not a location on a map. It is a description of a people, one heart and one mind, no poor among them. That kind of community does not emerge on its own. It gets built through proximity, through sacrifice, and through a shared pursuit of something real.

We gather because the Restoration is unfinished. Because the scriptures reward the kind of sustained, communal study that reading alone rarely produces. Because the people who find their way into this community, who resonated with these teachings and felt something shift when they encountered this theology, did not end up here by accident.

Remnants gather. This is where we gather.

The Zion Media Connection

The Zion Gathering grew directly out of the Zion Media community. The teachers and voices from the podcast and YouTube channel come to these gatherings to teach in person, in depth, without a time limit. If you have spent hours in that content wishing you could go further, ask your questions directly, and be part of the conversation, this is what we built for that.

If you have not yet found Zion Media, the conversations that birthed this community are still going every week. Visit the channel to hear them.

Who This Is For

People who take the Restoration seriously and are not satisfied with easy answers. People who have been studying on their own and are ready to study alongside others. People who believe what the prophets described about Zion and want to be part of a community actually moving in that direction.

You do not need a complete theology and you do not need to have watched everything. You just need to be genuinely hungry. If you are, you are exactly who this is for.

What To Expect

The location changes and the themes go deeper each time, but certain things stay constant. Rigorous scripture study, teaching from people who have spent years in the material, unhurried space for real questions and real conversation, worship, and activities that bring people together in ways that sessions alone never quite do.

Every gathering is fully all-inclusive. Your accommodations, your meals, your access to everything on the program are all covered from the moment you arrive. You bring an open heart and we handle everything else.
