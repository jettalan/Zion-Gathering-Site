“And even so will I gather mine elect from the four quarters of the earth, even as many as will believe in me, and hearken unto my voice.” — Doctrine and Covenants 33:6

Some of you do not want to miss a single gathering. You have felt what happens when the right people end up in the same room on sacred ground, and you have decided that wherever we gather next, you intend to be there. The Frequent Flyer Program is built for you.

Become a member once and you never register for a gathering again. When the next one is announced — your seat is already saved. You board before the public, your place is held, and you simply tell us you are coming. No scramble for spots, no waiting for the page to go live.

There are two tiers, split by how far you are willing to travel. Milestone keeps your seat saved at every gathering here in the States. Liahona — named for the compass that carried Lehi’s family across the sea to the promised land — puts you on every gathering on earth, anywhere we go, for as long as we gather.
