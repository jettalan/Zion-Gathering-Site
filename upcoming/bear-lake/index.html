The Monster Mansion at Bear Lake is not a typical retreat venue and this is not a typical retreat. We came here once, not entirely sure what to expect, and left knowing we had to come back. The property is stunning, the setting is wide open, and something about being away from everything ordinary seems to give people permission to go places they would not otherwise go.

This gathering is built for people who want to go deeper into the Restoration, study scripture with others who take it seriously, and spend a few days with the voices from Zion Media they have been listening to from a distance. Shane Baldwin, David Douglas, Latter Day Chad, Mciver, April Mciver, Dale Porter, James Skousen, Mike Rigby and others will all be there in person, teaching and spending time with the group throughout the weekend. The sessions are substantive and the conversations that happen outside the sessions are just as good.

What to Expect

  • Deep scripture study and doctrinal teaching from Zion Media presenters
  • Sessions focused on expanding and exploring restored doctrines
  • A ropes course and outdoor activities on the property
  • Suno, the Voice of Zion — an AI song creation and competition event that has to be seen to be believed
  • All meals, snacks, and drinks provided throughout the entire event
  • A swag bag waiting for you when you check in
  • Access to one of the most well-appointed private properties on Bear Lake
  • The kind of conversations that keep going long after the sessions end
