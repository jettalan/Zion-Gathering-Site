What Is The Zion Gathering

Some things cannot be streamed.

When a room full of people who have been searching for the same things finally ends up in the same place, something happens that a podcast cannot replicate. It takes presence. Shared meals. The kind of unhurried conversation that only happens when nobody is rushing home.

That is what The Zion Gathering is built around.

We bring together serious students of the Restoration who believe the scriptures hold more than they have been shown, that Zion is not a metaphor, and that becoming a people of one heart and one mind is worth actually working toward. Each gathering is built around deep teaching, genuine worship, and the kind of human connection that stays with people long after they drive home.

If you have been looking for something more than what you have found so far, you are in the right place.
