“He is like a refiner’s fire, and like fullers’ soap: and he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver: and he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness.” — Malachi 3:2–3

This October we are calling fathers to bring their sons to Bear Lake for a weekend focused on purification, sanctification, priesthood power, and becoming true servants of the Lord together. This is not just another retreat. It is a fathers-and-sons gathering centered on the refining of the sons of Levi — dads and their boys, in the same room, doing the deeper work side by side.

We will dive deep into the baptism of the Holy Ghost, exercising power in the priesthood, healing blessings and proper priesthood administration, sanctification as priesthood holders, the return of priesthood power, and the priesthood spoken of in Doctrine and Covenants 124 and how it has been restored. Fathers will learn to lead their sons in it; sons will learn what it means to carry it.

Bring a son, bring every son. There is no age restriction — young men and grown sons alike. If you desire to draw your family closer to the Lord, teach your boys how to access priesthood power, become sanctified as a servant, understand healing and blessings, or prepare your household for what is coming, this gathering is for you and yours.

What to Expect

  • Powerful scripture deep dives and doctrinal teaching from Zion Media presenters
  • Priesthood instruction — how to access it, how to administer it, how to pass it to your sons
  • A ropes course and outdoor activities on the property
  • Suno, the Voice of Zion — an AI song creation and competition event that has to be seen to be believed
  • All meals, snacks, and drinks provided throughout the entire event
  • A swag bag waiting for you at check-in
  • Worship and a sacrament-style meeting on Sunday
  • The kind of father-and-son conversations that keep going long after the sessions end

Check-in: Thursday evening between 5 and 6 PM. The full weekend schedule is released closer to the date.

The Venue

The Monster Mansion, on Bear Lake.

One of the most well-appointed private properties on Bear Lake — a stunning home in a wide-open setting, with room for the whole gathering under one roof. We have gathered here before, and we knew we had to come back.

Fathers and sons live, study, and play together for the weekend in one remarkable home — shared meals around long tables, sessions in the great room, and the kind of unhurried time together that ordinary life rarely allows.
